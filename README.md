# Pillie

An iOS pill reminder that actually helps you stay on track: reminders for your medication, plus a screen-time blocker that keeps distracting apps away until you have taken your dose.

Live on the App Store, with real users and real revenue. I build, ship, market, and support Pillie solo, including analytics and A/B testing on the funnel.

📲 [Download Pillie on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759352439)

**What it does**
- Daily reminders for medication and supplements, built around your real schedule
- App blocker: chosen apps stay blocked until you log your dose
- A 14-day in-app trial that counts active days only, so break days do not burn it

**Stack:** Swift / SwiftUI, PostHog analytics, RevenueCat subscriptions
