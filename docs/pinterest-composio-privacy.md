# Idriss Kone Pinterest Composio Connector Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 20, 2026

## 1. Overview

The Idriss Kone Composio Connector (the "Connector") is a personal-use integration that connects the account owner's Pinterest business account to Composio so authorized AI agents can perform requested Pinterest actions. This policy explains how Pinterest data is accessed and used through the Connector.

## 2. Data the Connector May Access

After the account owner authorizes access through Pinterest OAuth, the Connector may access data covered by the granted permissions, including:

- Pinterest profile and basic account information
- Pins, boards, board sections, and related account resources
- Account and Pin analytics
- Content and instructions supplied to create, update, move, or delete Pins and boards

The Connector does not receive or collect the account owner's Pinterest password.

## 3. How Data Is Used

Pinterest data is used only to carry out actions requested by the authenticated account owner, such as reading account content and analytics, searching the owner's content, and creating or managing the owner's Pins and boards. The Connector is not offered to external users and does not access general users' private account data.

## 4. Service Providers and Sharing

Composio processes authorization and Pinterest data as necessary to provide the Connector. Pinterest provides the source and destination services. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies:

- [Composio Privacy Policy](https://composio.dev/privacy)
- [Pinterest Privacy Policy](https://policy.pinterest.com/privacy-policy)

Pinterest data accessed through the Connector is not sold, rented, or used for targeted advertising. It is not shared with unrelated third parties.

## 5. Retention

Idriss Kone does not operate a separate database for Pinterest data used by this Connector. Authorization and data processing performed by Composio are subject to Composio's retention and security practices. Disconnecting the Pinterest account stops future access by the Connector. Content already created on Pinterest remains in the Pinterest account until the account owner changes or deletes it.

## 6. Security

The Connector uses Pinterest's OAuth authorization flow rather than collecting Pinterest passwords. Access is limited to the permissions approved by the account owner. No method of electronic processing is completely secure, but reasonable measures are used by the involved service providers to protect authorization data.

## 7. Account Controls and Deletion

The account owner can stop future access by disconnecting Pinterest in Composio or revoking the app's access in Pinterest account settings. For questions or deletion requests relating to this Connector, use the contact information below.

## 8. Children

The Connector is not intended for use by children under 13.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the Connector or its data practices change. Material updates will be reflected by changing the effective date above.
