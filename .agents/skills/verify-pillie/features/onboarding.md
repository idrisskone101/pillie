# Onboarding

The first-launch flow from Welcome to Home. Steps are `OnboardingFlow.Step` (`Services/OnboardingFlow.swift`); the live order a fresh install actually walks is `OnboardingFlow.displayOrder`, not the switch-case order in `ContentView.swift`. Welcome is its own small machine (`ProtectionPlanOnboardingShell`) that hands off to the first question.

## Sub-features

- `onboarding-welcome` — `ProtectionPlanWelcomeView`, "Get started".
- `onboarding-pain-points` — `ProtectionPlanDistractionChoicesView` (distraction + desired-outcome, both required).
- `onboarding-miss-frequency` — `ProtectionPlanFailureFrequencyView` (frequency + risk window, both required).
- `onboarding-acquisition-source` — `ProtectionPlanAcquisitionSourceView`, single choice or skip.
- `onboarding-method` — `ProtectionPlanRoutineMethodView`, pill/patch/ring, defaults to pill.
- `onboarding-today-pill` — pill users only: `TodayPillView` (`Views/Onboarding/TodayPill/`) on the `.schedule` step. Tap today's pill in a `PackCard`, answer "Have you taken pill N yet?", Continue. Writes only a `TodayPillPick` draft to UserDefaults; step 8 commits it (`TodayPillCommit`). The card's Change button (`todayPillChangeButton`) opens `PackTypeSheet`, one tile per preset (`packSheet.21_7` … `packSheet.21_4`) and a full-width Custom row (`packSheet.custom`) that swaps the sheet to `CustomPackEditor`: active/break steppers, a typed active count, sugar or no pills, and "Use N + M".
- `onboarding-dial` — patch and ring only: `RoutineDialView` (`Views/Onboarding/RoutineDial/`) on the `.schedule` step. Drag the knob (`routineDial`, one adjustable element) to today; patch picks Patch 1-3 / Off week first (`routineDialSegment.1`…`.4`). Days with a task (patch 1/8/15 on, 22 off; ring 1 in, 22 out) ask a question (`routineDialQuestion`, `routineDialYes`, `routineDialNotYet`, then `routineDialStatus`); other days show `routineDialResult`. Writes only a `RoutineDialPick` draft (`pillie_onboarding_routine_dial_pick`); step 8 commits it through `TodayPillCommit`.
- `onboarding-reminder-time` — `ProtectionPlanReminderTimeView`; its CTA saves the time, commits the pill pick with it, then requests real notification authorization. The pill path's plan card reads Pack / Today ("Pill 12 · taken") / Next reminder.
- `onboarding-reminder-plan` — `ProtectionPlanDiagnosisView`, an analyze-then-reveal animation before the CTA appears.
- `onboarding-trial-granted` — `TrialGrantedMomentView`. Dead code: unreachable by any real path or debug deep link (see Gotchas).
- `onboarding-app-blocking` — the `appBlocking` step, one of three screens by access (`BlockingSetupAccess`, `Views/Onboarding/BlockingSetup/`). With Plus access it is the rehearsal, `BlockingRehearsalView`: three beats that play themselves if she waits (reminder lands, app paused, shake to check in), then the ask, then her picks, or the refused screen after Apple's "Don't Allow". Without access it is `BlockingSetupLockedView`. All of the journey's rules are in `BlockingRehearsalState`.
- `onboarding-protection-plan-ready` — `ProtectionPlanReadyView`, "Go to Today".
- `onboarding-home-handoff` — lands on `MainTabView` (Today).

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Fresh install. Tap "Get started".
- Answer the two consolidated question screens (pick one distraction + one desired outcome; one miss-frequency + one risk window), then Where did you find Pillie? (or "Not now"), method, then tap today's pill and answer (pill) or drag the dial to today (patch/ring), reminder time (allow or deny the real notification prompt), then the reminder-plan reveal.
- Watch or play the rehearsal: tap the featured app (her first Distractions answer among TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Messages), tap "Open Pillie", shake once or tap "Tap to check in instead". Each beat plays itself after 3.5 s, 3 s and 4 s when untouched. There is no Skip.
- Tap "Choose what to pause". A real device shows Apple's Screen Time alert and then the FamilyControls picker; the simulator cannot refuse or pick (see Gotchas). After "Don't Allow" the screen offers "Try again" and "Use reminders only". After Allow, an ask with nothing picked offers "Use reminders only" too, because Apple's prompt is no longer a way to say no.
- If a valid blocker config saves, "You’re all set." → "Go to Today" lands on Home. "Use reminders only" goes straight to Home instead.

## Driving it with flows

- `flows/onboarding-walkthrough.flow` — `fresh`, `launch`, then every reachable step to Home with each step's real CTA. `trialGranted` is skipped: it cannot be shown by any path, real or debug (see Gotchas). It touches nothing on the rehearsal, so the three beats play themselves: this is the self-play pass on a real fresh install. Reaches the picks via `pillie://debug/plus-app-blocking-setup?selected=N` (FamilyControls tokens cannot be faked any other way on the simulator), then taps that screen's real "Continue".
- `flows/onboarding-pack-sheet.flow` — lands on the pill step with `defaults onboardingStep -int 10`, opens the pack type sheet, switches 21 + 7 to 24 + 4 keeping the tapped pill, then 26 + 2 to 21 + 4 clearing pill 26.
- `flows/onboarding-dial.flow` — patch and ring step 7 with `defaults onboardingStep -int 10` plus `pillie_contraceptive_method`: the put-on and remove questions, Yes and Not yet status lines, and result rows seeded from `-data` drafts. It moves the dial through the segment picker and drafts only; dragging needs coordinates from a fresh shot.
- `flows/onboarding-custom-pack.flow` — opens the Custom row from the pack sheet, steps and types the editor to 88 + 3 (grid, then the long-pack map, then typing 100), taps Use, and answers a pill on the paged 91-day card.
- `flows/onboarding-trial-reminders.flow` — `fresh`, walks onboarding to the app-blocking step, then dumps the pending notification queue (`pillie://debug/dump-pending-notifications`) into `app.log` with no apps chosen. Proves the first plan after the trial grant holds the smart follow-ups, and the day-10/13 warnings plus the day-15 expiry-day notice (ENG-141). A fresh install is post-cutover, so those notices carry the hard-paywall pick-a-plan bodies and never say daily reminders stay free (ENG-161). The blocking-cohort copy is not reachable on the simulator, see the flow header.
- `flows/onboarding-blocking-stages.flow` — every stage of the rehearsal and the locked screen, each opened by `pillie://debug/plus-app-blocking-setup?stage=…` without walking onboarding first: the three beats held still, the ask before Apple's prompt (`access=notAsked`) and after Allow, with and without the trial line, the picks with 4, 8 and 20 sample tiles, the refused screen for both reasons, and the locked screen (`terms=hard&expired=1`). Add `&lang=<code>` to every link for a locale pass.
- `flows/onboarding-blocking-live.flow` — `fresh`, then the rehearsal played twice with `log start`: once by hand, once untouched (`selfPlay=on`), then the refusal path through "Try again" and "Use reminders only" to Today, then "Use reminders only" from the ask after Allow. Read `app.log` for the `blocking_rehearsal_*`, `screen_time_permission_*` and `blocker_setup_skipped` events; the flow header lists what must be there.
- `flows/onboarding-frames.flow` — perf. Sets `PillieFrameMonitor`, then taps through painPoints, missFrequency, acquisitionSource, method, and both dials, with three scrubs per dial. `TabSwitchFrameProbe` logs a window per step change (`go:<step>-<method>`), per settled screen (`idle:…`), per row or chip tap, and per scrub. `Pillie/scripts/onboarding-frames.py app.log [app.log …]` tabulates dropped frames and the worst frame per label. A screen's first visit costs more than later visits, so compare runs of the whole flow.
- `flows/onboarding-render-load.flow` — perf. painPoints and missFrequency six times each way, then the settled screen. Run it through `Pillie/scripts/render-server-load.sh onboarding-render-load 20 'note render-start'`, which samples the simulator's `backboardd` and prints render-thread busy ms and the time inside shadows and blurs. That is a proxy for device GPU work: compare builds against each other, never against a device.
- Both perf flows take `PILLIE_APP_PATH` (honored by `sim-flow.sh`) to run a Release build compiled with `SWIFT_ACTIVE_COMPILATION_CONDITIONS='$(inherited) PILLIE_FRAME_PROBE'` and empty `POSTHOG_PROJECT_TOKEN` / `APPSFLYER_DEV_KEY`, so no analytics leave the simulator.
- Two other debug deep links land mid-flow without a fresh install: `pillie://debug/onboarding-personalization-intent` (painPoints) and `pillie://debug/onboarding-personalization-timing` (missFrequency) — `PillieApp.swift` ~557-566.
- Unreachable, not covered by any flow, and not a gap in their coverage: `trialGranted` (dead code — see Gotchas) and the analyzing (pre-reveal) beat of the diagnosis screen (timing-dependent, and the finished plan is the state worth proving).

## Gotchas

- **The pill path writes nothing to PillStore until the reminder-time CTA.** `TodayPillView` saves a `TodayPillPick` draft (`pillie_onboarding_today_pill_pick`); `OnboardingReminderCommit` commits it after saving the hour/minute and before the permission request, so the tapped pill lands on the right dose window. Patch and ring work the same way with a `RoutineDialPick`. A user resumed on `reminderTime` without a draft for the chosen method is sent back to `.schedule` (`OnboardingFlow.visibleStep(needsTodayPick:)`). With an evening reminder, "Not yet" before 8 PM shows the previous pill logged on Home until the reminder opens pill N (ADR 0009's live day).
- **Tap the welcome CTA only after the splash clears.** `launch` returns while the splash is fading and the first tap can be dropped; the walkthrough sleeps 2 s first. If every tap in a flow is ignored (even Done on a debug sheet), reboot the simulator: axe's HID connection goes stale.

- **Most plan-builder screens share two identifiers**, set once in `ProtectionPlanScaffold.swift:198,219`: `protectionPlanPrimaryCTA` (Continue) and `protectionPlanSecondaryCTA` (Skip/Not now, where present). Per-choice rows/chips usually have no id — tap by their exact English label (from the xcstrings key cited in code) unless the screen sets one explicitly, as `ProtectionPlanAcquisitionSourceView.swift:68` does with `acquisitionSource.<rawValue>` (e.g. `acquisitionSource.tiktok`).
- **`trialGranted` cannot be shown at all — not even through its own debug deep link.** `ContentView`'s switch selects on `OnboardingFlow.visibleStep(for: onboardingStep, ...)` (`ContentView.swift:86-90`), and that function unconditionally migrates `.trialGranted` (and `.paywall`, `.freePlanConfirmation`, `.mechanismProof`, and the other retired steps) to `.appBlocking` before returning (`OnboardingFlow.swift:316-326`) — so the switch's `.trialGranted` case (`TrialGrantedMomentView`, `trialGrantedPrimaryCTA`) never runs, no matter what raw value `onboardingStep` holds. `pillie://debug/trial-granted-moment` (`PillieApp.swift:567-573`) sets `onboardingStep` to that raw value anyway, but the very next render migrates it straight past — the deep link's own code comment says as much ("the retired step migrates to app-blocking setup"). It is a same-effect alias for landing on `appBlocking` with the entitlement forced off first, not a way to see the trial-granted screen. Do not put it in a flow expecting `TrialGrantedMomentView` to render.
- **The rehearsal's ids.** Beat 1: `blockingRehearsalBanner`, `blockingRehearsalFeaturedApp`. Beat 2: `blockingRehearsalPauseCard`, `blockingRehearsalOpenPillie`. Beat 3: `blockingRehearsalShakeBeat` (the stage), `blockingRehearsalTapInstead`. The ask: `blockingRehearsalCheckedInPill`. Picks: `blockingRehearsalPicksCard` (the tiles), `blockingRehearsalChangePicks`. Refused: `blockingRehearsalPromptPreview` (absent when another app holds Screen Time), `blockingRehearsalRemindersOnly`. The footer button on every stage that has one is `blockingRehearsalPrimary`, and the trial line is `appBlockingTrialLine`. The locked screen's card is `blockingSetupLockedCard`.
- **A beat on a real walk does not wait for a flow.** It plays itself after a few seconds, so a `tap` on a beat's control races the demo. A flow that needs to tap through opens `stage=reminder` first: a seeded beat holds still until `selfPlay=on`. A flow that only passes through waits for `blockingRehearsalBanner`, then seeds `selected=N`.
- **The simulator cannot shake, refuse Screen Time, or pick apps.** A tap on `blockingRehearsalShakeBeat` counts as the one shake (simulator builds only). `isAuthorized` is `true` on the simulator (`AppBlockingManager.updateAuthorizationStatus`) until `pillie://debug/screen-time-refused?reason=cancelled|conflict` or `stage=refused` forces a refusal; clear it with `reason=none`, or the next flow in the same launch is refused too. So a simulator ask is always the ask after Allow (no Apple line, "Use reminders only" offered). `access=notAsked` on the stage link draws the first-time ask instead; the next "Choose what to pause" then counts as allowed. Picks are sample tiles from `debugSelectionCountOverride` (`selected=N`); the real tiles are `Label(token)`, drawn by iOS, and need a device.
- **"Demo seen" is persisted** (`onboarding_blocking_rehearsal_seen`). After the beats have played once, a relaunch or a return to the step opens on the ask. `fresh` clears it; `stage=reminder` ignores it.
- **The reminder-time CTA ("Continue to notification settings") requests real notification authorization** (`OnboardingReminderCommit.swift`, wired live in `ProtectionPlanReminderTimeView.swift:178-187`) — unlike FamilyControls, this is not simulator-shortcut and shows the real iOS system permission alert on first ask post-`fresh`. The alert's button text ("Allow"/"Don't Allow") is iOS chrome, not Pillie copy — confirm the exact label live before trusting a scripted tap on it.
- The diagnosis screen (`reminderPlan`) holds its CTA back until the plan is "verified" (`ProtectionPlanDiagnosisView.swift:100-119`); wait for `protectionPlanPrimaryCTA` with a longer timeout, not the screen's title text, which is present during the analyzing beat too.
- Coordinates/labels are English (`?lang=en` on every debug deep link, or confirm the simulator's base language before a `fresh` with no deep link).
